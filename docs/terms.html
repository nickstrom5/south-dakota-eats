Terms of use

The plain-language terms for the South Dakota Eats app and this website. Last updated 2026-11-06.

What the app is

South Dakota Eats is a free guide to restaurants in South Dakota. It is provided as is, for personal use, without charge and without warranties of any kind.

Check before you go

Restaurants open, close, change their hours and change their menus, and seasonal places keep seasons. Dishes and founding years are what each place or a named source said when we checked in fall 2026. We work to keep the lists right, but we can't promise that any listing is current or complete. Call the restaurant before you make the trip.

Inspections and public records

Inspection scores in the app and on this site are the City of Sioux Falls' own, as the City publishes them, and dated. The South Dakota Department of Health's reports are search-only, so none is shown for places elsewhere; they link to its search. We compute no grade, and the two systems are never blended. For official records, ask the City or the Department of Health.
